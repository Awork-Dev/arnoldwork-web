Every question you ask yourself in the weight room, answered in seconds, no sign-up.

ArnoldWork brings together 20 free tools to train smart, made by someone who has been lifting since 1996.

💪 STRENGTH
• Your one-rep max (1RM) and what weight to use for each percentage
• Should I add weight? It tells you when
• Which plates to load, in kilos, pounds or mixed
• Warm-up sets, strength level, RIR and RPE, DOTS and Wilks points

🍗 NUTRITION AND BODY
• Calories and macros, protein and creatine
• Body fat and FFMI, muscle potential and goal date

📋 PLAN AND LOG
• Routine generator for your days, level and gym
• Training log with progress charts, export to PDF, TXT or your calendar
• Routine quiz, weekly volume, exercise swaps, rest timer and tempo

✅ NO STRINGS
• 100% free • No ads • No in-app purchases • No sign-up • Works offline
