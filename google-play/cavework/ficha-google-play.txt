SHORT DESCRIPTION
Smash dinos, eat protein and lift heavier across 8 worlds.

FULL DESCRIPTION
You're a caveman with a stone dumbbell and one mission: lift heavier and heavier.

Run non-stop, smash dinosaurs, eat the protein they drop and fill the bar to add more weight. But watch out: protein drains while you run. Stop eating and you run out of strength.

🌍 8 WORLDS
Every 20 kg the world changes, with its own scenery, weather and dangers: the Jungle, the Great Plains, the Swamp, the Ice Age, the Caves, the Red Desert, the Volcano and Iron Olympus. How far can you go?

🦖 BOSSES
The short-armed T-Rex and the mammoth show up every few levels. Wait for them to tire out, then strike.

💪 POWER-UPS
Protein shake magnet, lifting belt shield, unstoppable pre-workout and MAKINA mode with double points.

🏆 WORLDWIDE RANKING
All-time, weekly and daily Top 50, monthly seasons, leagues with your friends and a daily challenge that's the same for everyone.
